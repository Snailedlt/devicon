# About the Maintainers, Supporters, and Contributors  

Devicon is a collaborative open-source project driven by a diverse group of dedicated individuals. Here’s an overview of the roles that make this project possible:  

## Maintainers  
The maintainers are responsible for the overall direction and management of the project. They ensure the repository is well-organized, review contributions, and address issues to maintain the project's quality.  

### Responsibilities:  
- Reviewing and merging pull requests.  
- Managing project milestones and updates.  
- Maintaining documentation and ensuring alignment with community standards.  

## Supporters  
Supporters are those who help the project thrive through advocacy, promotion, or providing financial and technical support. Their contributions help Devicon reach a broader audience and improve the resources available for the community.  

### Contributions:  
- Promoting Devicon through social media and other platforms.  
- Providing resources like hosting or funding for tools.  
- Offering advice and feedback to guide the project’s growth.  

## Contributors  
Contributors are the backbone of Devicon, offering their time and expertise to improve the project. This includes adding new icons, fixing bugs, enhancing documentation, and sharing creative ideas.  

### How to Contribute:  
1. Check the [Contribution Guidelines](https://github.com/devicons/devicon?tab=readme-ov-file#contribute) for details on how to get started.  
2. Look for issues labeled **"Good First Issue"** or **"Help Wanted"** in the repository.  
3. Share your work by submitting a pull request!  

## Acknowledgments  
We’re deeply grateful to all maintainers, supporters, and contributors who have helped Devicon become what it is today. Your efforts make this project a valuable resource for developers worldwide.  

### Join Us!  
Want to get involved? Whether you’re a developer, designer, or just a fan of the project, there’s always room for more collaboration. Explore the repository and start contributing today!
