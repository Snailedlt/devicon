In order to create our font icons, we utilize [Icomoon](https://icomoon.io/app/#/select) and its wonderful font creation service. However, due to this dependency, many of our icon requirements are based off of Icomoon. This includes not accepting any strokes or texts in the SVGs before the icon creation process.

If you are interested in the icon creation process, check out their [documentation](https://icomoon.io/#docs/importing) and [site](https://icomoon.io/app/#/select). 

If you are want to customize our font files (e.g you only want 10 icons instead of our 150+ icons), you can upload the `icomoon.json` file using the `Import Icons` button. You can then choose the icons you want to keep and follow the link above to generate your icons.
